Privacy Policy

Data Collection & Storage

ZZZ Optimizer is committed to protecting your privacy. This tool operates entirely within your web browser and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

Local Storage Only

All data you enter (character builds, disc drives, W-Engines, etc.) is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device and is only accessible to you.

No Server Communication

This application does not send any data to external servers. All calculations and optimizations are performed entirely on your device.

No Tracking or Analytics

We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or any tracking mechanisms. Your usage of this tool is completely private and anonymous.

No User Accounts

This tool does not require user accounts or authentication. You can use it without providing any personal information.

Your Data Control

Since all data is stored locally in your browser, you have complete control over it:

  • Export Your Data: Use the Data Manager to export all your builds and configurations
  • Delete Your Data: Clear your browser's local storage or use the Data Manager to remove specific items
  • Backup Your Data: Export your data regularly to ensure you don't lose your builds if you clear browser data

Third-Party Resources

This application uses game assets and data from publicly available sources. When you visit this site, your browser may load resources from:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting platform)
  • Game asset CDNs (for character images and icons)

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies. Please refer to their respective websites for more information.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page. Since we don't collect any contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes.
