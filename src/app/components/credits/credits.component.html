How This Works

Assumptions

To provide consistent calculations and scoring, this optimizer makes the following assumptions:

  • All Discs: Assumed to be S-Rank and upgraded to +15
  • All Agents: Assumed to be Level 60 with Core Skill/Passive maxed out
  • W-Engine/Disc Effects: Assumed to meet best-case scenario conditions for stat bonuses

My Philosophy

This tool provides estimates only and is not indicative of your skill or the true quality of your builds. I intentionally do not enforce "optimal" W-Engines or disc sets for each agent because I believe in player creativity and experimentation.

Skill expression is what makes ZZZ awesome! I don't want to put players in boxes or limit your creativity. Use this tool as a guide, but always play your way and have fun discovering unique strategies.

About This Project

ZZZ Optimizer is a community-driven tool designed to help Zenless Zone Zero players optimize their character builds, manage disc sets, and calculate stats efficiently.

Please keep in mind that is a constant work in progress. I am doing my best to update the scoring aglorithm whenever I can and making improvements to the site itself. Right now, it is just me making changes, so any sort of feedback might take a second to implement. Thank you for visiting and your patience with me!
