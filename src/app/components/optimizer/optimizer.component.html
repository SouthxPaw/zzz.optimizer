How Scoring Works

A breakdown of how discs and full builds are evaluated

📊

How Build Scoring Works

Build scores are calculated using a composite system that measures how functional, efficient, and well-optimized a build is for a specific agent — not just raw stat totals.

Composite Build Score Breakdown:

  1. Breakpoint Score (40%):
    • Each agent has important stat thresholds (breakpoints) they want to reach
    • Meeting these breakpoints grants full credit
    • Falling short reduces score
    • Additional stats continue to contribute normally; there are no diminishing returns in the current system
  2. Disc Quality Score (30%):
    • Uses the average quality score of the 6 equipped discs
    • Rewards efficient substat rolls into the agent’s priority stats
    • Penalizes wasted rolls and low-quality discs
  3. Stat Efficiency Score (20%):
    • Rewards clean, well-balanced stat allocation and prioritizing the right substats
    • Penalizes inefficient or poorly prioritized stat distributions
  4. Set Bonus Alignment (10%):
    • Evaluates how well equipped 2-piece and 4-piece sets match the agent’s needs
    • Optimal sets score highest, acceptable alternatives score partially

Additional Notes:

  • W-Engine and Mindscape stats are included with reduced impact (0.25× weight)
  • Builds are only compared within the same agent, W-Engine, and Mindscape context

How Individual Disc Scoring Works:

  1. Main Stat Bonus:
    • Each disc slot has preferred main stats for the agent
    • Matching a preferred main stat grants a flat bonus
  2. Substat Roll Evaluation:
    • Substats are scored by roll count, not raw stat value
    • Each roll into a priority stat increases the disc’s score
    • Rolls into non-priority stats are treated as wasted
  3. Priority Substat Bonus:
    • Having the correct substats matters more than roll count alone
    • 4/4 priority substats grants a large bonus
    • 3/4 priority substats grants a strong bonus
  4. High Roll Bonus:
    • Substats with 5–6 rolls are considered high-roll
    • Multiple high-roll priority stats grant additional bonuses
  5. Final Disc Score:
    • Total effective rolls are scaled to a 0–100+ score
    • Scores are not capped — exceptional “god rolls” can exceed 100

💡 Important: The optimizer prioritizes correct stats, breakpoint completion, and efficient roll usage. A build with slightly lower totals can score higher if it is more optimized and wastes fewer rolls.
