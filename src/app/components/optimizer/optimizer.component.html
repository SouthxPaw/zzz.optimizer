How Scoring Works

A breakdown of how discs and full builds are evaluated

📊

How Build Scoring Works

Build scores are calculated using a composite system that measures how functional, efficient, and well-optimized a build is for a specific agent, not just raw stat totals.

BUILD and DISC Ratings

  • VOID HUNTER (VH), PHAETHON (PHT), SSS, SS, S, A, B, C, D, F

Composite Build Score Breakdown:

  1. Disc Quality Score (75%) - MOST IMPORTANT:
    • Uses the average disc score of all 6 equipped discs
    • Each disc is scored individually from 0-144 points based on substats and rolls
    • VOID HUNTER-tier discs score 134+ points, PHAETHON scores 117+, SSS scores 105+, while average B-tier discs score 62-75 points
    • The average of all 6 disc scores determines this component - this is by far THE most critical factor for your build rating
    • This is the most impactful component at 75% of total build score
    • Scoring formula: Main Stat Points (0-3) + Substat Roll Points (rolls × weight × 3.0) + Roll Bonus (1pt if 5+ upgrade rolls). Priority stats have weights of 0.7-1.3×, while wasted stats get only 0.17× weight.
    • Why build ratings can exceed 100%: Individual discs can achieve exceptional ratings (VH = 110 points, PHT = 105 points) based on perfect substats and high rolls. Since the Disc Quality component accounts for 75% of your build rating, having multiple VOID HUNTER-tier discs can push the total score above 100%. For example: 6 VH discs (110 avg) × 0.75 weight = 82.5 points from discs alone. Add breakpoints (5%), stat efficiency (15%), and damage (5%), and you can exceed 100%. This is intentional! It rewards truly exceptional builds that go beyond "perfect" with outstanding RNG.
  2. Breakpoint Score (5%):
    • Each agent has important stat thresholds (breakpoints) they want to reach
    • Meeting minimum breakpoints grants partial credit (50%)
    • Meeting optimal breakpoints grants full credit (100%)
    • Progressive scaling between min and optimal (50%-100% based on how close to optimal)
    • Missing breakpoints applies penalties (-30% if below minimum on priority stats)
    • Priority stats are weighted 1.5× more heavily than non-priority stats
  3. Stat Efficiency Score (15%):
    • Rewards balanced investment in priority stats (up to +20 points for high coverage)
    • Penalizes wasted investment in non-priority stats (e.g., DEF% on DPS, ATK% on support) - up to -15 points
    • Bonus for meeting multiple priority stats: +10 for 3+ priority stats, +5 for 2 priority stats
    • Rewards exceeding optimal breakpoints with diminishing returns (+3 points for first 10% excess)
    • This reflects your building skill - investing in the right stats, not just stacking one stat!
  4. Damage Output Score (5%):
    • Estimates damage using actual ZZZ damage formulas
    • Includes agent passive bonuses (CRIT Rate, ATK%, DMG bonuses from agent's kit)
    • Includes agent scoring modifiers (DEF Shred, RES Shred, DMG bonuses)
    • Includes W-Engine scoring modifiers (buffs, debuffs, daze bonuses)
    • Example: Nicole's DEF Shred (40%) + RES Shred (25%) = +100% team damage!
    • Note: You can toggle W-Engine, Mindscape, and Passive bonuses on/off in the Character Builds tab

Additional Notes:

  • W-Engine and Mindscape stats are included with reduced impact (0.25× weight)
  • Builds are only compared within the same agent, W-Engine, and Mindscape context

Custom Upgrade Plans:

  • Create your own scoring profiles: In the "Upgrade Plans" tab, you can create custom plans that completely override the default stat weights and breakpoints for any agent
  • Full control: Set your own substat priorities (Essential/Important/Nice/Ignore), main stat preferences, and custom breakpoint targets
  • Per-agent customization: Each agent can have multiple upgrade plans with different goals (e.g., "Max CRIT Build" vs "Anomaly Build")
  • Apply to builds: In the "My Builds" tab, select an active upgrade plan from the dropdown - all disc and build scores will immediately reflect your custom priorities
  • Default option always available: Select "(Default)" in the dropdown to use the site's built-in stat weights and breakpoints
  • Export/Import: Share upgrade plans with others or backup your custom configurations

How Individual Disc Scoring Works:

  1. Main Stat Points (0-3):
    • Each disc slot has preferred main stats for the build type
    • Matching a preferred main stat: 3 points
    • Off-meta main stat on Drive 4/5/6: 0 points
    • Drive 1/2/3 always get 3 points (fixed main stats)
  2. Substat Roll Points (rolls × weight × 3.0):
    • Each substat is scored by: Total Rolls × Stat Weight × 3.0
    • Roll Count: Total rolls in the substat (1-6 rolls: 1 initial + up to 5 upgrades)
    • Stat Weight: How valuable the stat is for this build (0.7-1.3× multiplier)
    • Example: 5 rolls into CRIT Rate (weight 1.0) = 5 × 1.0 × 3.0 = 15 points
    • Example: 3 rolls into ATK% (weight 0.85) = 3 × 0.85 × 3.0 = 7.65 points
    • Example: 4 rolls into PEN (weight 0.7) = 4 × 0.7 × 3.0 = 8.4 points
    • Wasted stats (weight 0.17): 4 rolls × 0.17 × 3.0 = 2.04 points (heavily penalized!)
  3. Stat Weight Tiers:
    • Essential Stats (×1.3): Highest priority stats (e.g., CRIT Rate for Miyabi on CRIT main stats)
    • Core Stats (×1.0): Essential stats the agent needs (e.g., CRIT DMG for CRIT agents)
    • Important Stats (×0.85): Very good stats that amplify effectiveness (e.g., ATK%)
    • Nice Stats (×0.7): Useful but less impactful stats (e.g., PEN for some agents)
    • Wasted Stats (×0.17): Off-meta stats that barely contribute (e.g., DEF% on DPS agents)
  4. Roll Bonus (0 or 1 point):
    • If the disc has 5+ upgrade rolls (not counting initial substats), add +1 point
    • This rewards fully upgraded discs at +15
  5. Final Disc Score:
    • Formula: (Main Stat Points + Substat Roll Points + Roll Bonus) × 4.8
    • The score is calculated on a 0-30+ scale, then multiplied by 4.8 for the final rating
    • VOID HUNTER (134+): Needs ~28+ internal points (example: 3 main + 24 substat points + 1 bonus = 28, then × 4.8 = 134.4)
    • Example calculation: CRIT Rate (5 rolls × 1.0 × 3.0) + CRIT DMG (3 rolls × 1.0 × 3.0) = 15 + 9 = 24 substat points. Add 3 (main) + 1 (bonus) = 28 × 4.8 = 134.4 (VOID HUNTER!)
    • The system rewards both getting the RIGHT stats (high weights) and rolling them heavily (many rolls into priority stats)

💡 Important: The optimizer prioritizes correct stats, breakpoint completion, and efficient roll usage. A disc with perfect core stats will score higher than one with mediocre rolls into situational stats. The scoring system is designed to be honest—average discs score average, god rolls score exceptionally high.
