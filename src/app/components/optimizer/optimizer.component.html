How Scoring Works

A breakdown of how discs and full builds are evaluated

📊

How Build Scoring Works

Build scores are calculated using a composite system that measures how functional, efficient, and well-optimized a build is for a specific agent, not just raw stat totals.

Composite Build Score Breakdown:

  1. Disc Quality Score (50%) - MOST IMPORTANT:
    • Uses the average disc score of all 6 equipped discs
    • Each disc is scored individually from 0-170+ points based on substats
    • God-tier discs can score 150+ points, while average discs score ~100
    • The average of all 6 disc scores determines this component
    • This is the most impactful component at 50% of total build score
    • Diminishing returns: Stacking too much of one stat (like 50% CRIT Rate) gives less value than spreading stats evenly. A balanced disc with 20% CRIT + 15% ATK% scores better than one with 40% CRIT alone.
  2. Breakpoint Score (20%):
    • Each agent has important stat thresholds (breakpoints) they want to reach
    • Meeting minimum breakpoints grants partial credit (50%+)
    • Meeting optimal breakpoints grants full credit (100%)
    • Missing breakpoints applies penalties (-30% below min, -15% between min and optimal)
    • Priority stats are weighted 1.5× more heavily than non-priority stats
  3. Stat Efficiency Score (15%):
    • Rewards clean, well-balanced stat allocation and prioritizing the right substats
    • Penalizes inefficient or poorly prioritized stat distributions
    • Higher weight than damage because this reflects your building skill
  4. Damage Output Score (10%):
    • Estimates damage using actual ZZZ damage formulas
    • Includes agent passive bonuses (CRIT Rate, ATK%, DMG bonuses from agent's kit)
    • Includes agent scoring modifiers (DEF Shred, RES Shred, DMG bonuses)
    • Includes W-Engine scoring modifiers (buffs, debuffs, daze bonuses)
    • Example: Nicole's DEF Shred (40%) + RES Shred (25%) = +100% team damage!
    • Note: You can toggle W-Engine, Mindscape, and Passive bonuses on/off in the Character Builds tab
  5. Set Bonus Alignment (5%):
    • Evaluates how well equipped 2-piece and 4-piece sets match the agent's needs
    • Optimal sets score highest, acceptable alternatives score partially

Additional Notes:

  • W-Engine and Mindscape stats are included with reduced impact (0.25× weight)
  • Builds are only compared within the same agent, W-Engine, and Mindscape context

How Individual Disc Scoring Works:

  1. Main Stat Bonus:
    • Each disc slot has preferred main stats for the agent
    • Matching a preferred main stat grants a flat bonus
  2. Substat Evaluation (Roll Count × Stat Weight × Quality):
    • Substats are scored by three factors working together:
    • Roll Count: Total rolls in the substat (1-6 rolls: 1 initial + up to 5 upgrades)
    • Stat Weight: How important the stat is for the agent (0.7-1.0 multiplier)
    • Quality Multiplier: How efficiently the stat rolled (0.7-1.0 based on roll count)
    • Example: 5 rolls into CRIT Rate (×1.0 weight, ×1.0 quality) = 5.0 points
    • Example: 3 rolls into PEN (×0.7 weight, ×0.88 quality) = 1.85 points
    • Rolls into non-priority stats are treated as wasted (0 points)
  3. Stat Weight Tiers:
    • Core Stats (×1.0): Essential stats the agent needs (e.g., CRIT Rate for agents who use/need Crit Value)
    • Strong Stats (×0.85): Very good stats that amplify effectiveness (e.g., ATK%)
    • Situational Stats (×0.7): Useful but less impactful stats (e.g., PEN for some agents who can use it but not need it)
  4. Priority Substat Bonus:
    • Having the correct substats matters more than roll count alone
    • 4/4 priority substats grants +12 effective rolls (no wasted stats)
    • 3/4 priority substats grants +8 effective rolls (only one wasted stat)
  5. High Roll Bonus:
    • Substats with 4-5 rolls are considered high-roll
    • 2+ high-roll priority stats grant +4 points per substat
    • 1 high-roll priority stat grants +5 points
  6. Final Disc Score:
    • Total effective rolls (weighted + bonuses) are scaled to a 0–100+ score
    • Scores are not capped! Exceptional "god rolls" can exceed 100!
    • The system rewards both getting the RIGHT stats and rolling them efficiently

💡 Important: The optimizer prioritizes correct stats, breakpoint completion, and efficient roll usage. A disc with perfect core stats will score higher than one with mediocre rolls into situational stats. The scoring system is designed to be honest—average discs score average, god rolls score exceptionally high.
