How This Works

Assumptions

To provide consistent calculations and scoring, this optimizer makes the following assumptions:

  • All Discs: Assumed to be S-Rank and upgraded to +15
  • All Agents: Assumed to be Level 60 with Core Skill/Passive maxed out
  • W-Engine Effects: Assumed to meet best-case scenario conditions for stat bonuses

Our Philosophy

This tool provides estimates only and is not indicative of your skill or the true quality of your builds. We intentionally do not enforce "optimal" W-Engines or disc sets for each agent because we believe in player creativity and experimentation.

Skill expression is what makes ZZZ awesome! We don't want to put players in boxes or limit your creativity. Use this tool as a guide, but always play your way and have fun discovering unique strategies.

About This Project

ZZZ Optimizer is a community-driven tool designed to help Zenless Zone Zero players optimize their character builds, manage disc sets, and calculate stats efficiently.

This is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HoYoverse. All game assets and data belong to their respective owners.
